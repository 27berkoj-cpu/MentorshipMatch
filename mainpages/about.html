About MentorshipMatch

What is MentorshipMatch?

Mentorship match is a web application designed to connect high school and college students with mentors in their field of interest.

With the rise of artificial intelligence, there is less respect for college degrees and education, and a less than promising job market. It is important for students to have access to resources that give them real world experiences that can help them reach their respective career goals.

Here at MentorshipMatch, we believe that everyone deserves access to quality mentorship opportunities, regardless of their background or circumstances.

How it works

MentorshipMatch has a simple process for students and mentors to connect and collaborate.

  1. Create a profile

    Students can share their academic interests, career goals, and a description of themselves as they create their profiles.

    Mentors can share their expertise, experience, and employment information to connect with students.

  2. Find and request a mentor

    Students can browse mentors in their field of interest. Once they find a mentor they like, they can send a request to connect.

  3. Schedule and attend a session

    Once a student and mentor have matched, the student can schedule and attend a session with their mentor to gain the mentor's guidance.

  4. Provide reviews and feedback

    After each session, students and mentors can provide reviews and feedback about their experience, helping create a safe and supportive environment for learning and career exploration.

  5. Continue to learn and grow

    Once a student has successfully paired with a mentor, they can continue to learn and grow through their ongoing interactions.
